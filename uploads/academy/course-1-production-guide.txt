DIGITAL SMART CLASS
YouTube Content Creation & Video Production

Creator workbook

Use this workbook to plan one original video from idea to upload.

1. Audience and promise
- Who is the video for?
- What specific problem will it solve?
- Write one sentence that promises a useful result without clickbait.

2. Pre-production
- Write a hook for the first 15 seconds.
- Create a three-part outline: setup, demonstration, conclusion.
- Prepare only music, images, fonts, and footage that you created or licensed.

3. Production checklist
- Record clean audio before chasing cinematic visuals.
- Lock exposure and focus when using a phone.
- Capture wide, medium, close-up, and cutaway shots.
- Record room tone for clean edits.

4. Editing checklist
- Remove pauses that do not help the story.
- Add readable captions and descriptive on-screen text.
- Keep music below speech and credit every licensed asset.
- Export a watchable 1080p file and review it on a phone.

5. Copyright reminder
Do not upload clips, music, images, or videos you did not make or license. A short clip is not automatically fair use. Keep receipts, licence links, and attribution records for every third-party asset.

Final deliverable
Submit the unlisted video URL, a one-paragraph production reflection, and an asset credit list.
