DIGITAL SMART CLASS
YouTube Monetization & Online Business

Business planning workbook

1. Monetization foundations
Separate audience growth from revenue. Review current YouTube Partner Program eligibility directly in YouTube Help because requirements and available features can change.

2. Revenue mix
Evaluate advertising revenue, memberships, sponsorships, affiliate links, digital products, services, and licensing. Select only offers that genuinely help your audience.

3. Unit economics
Estimate monthly views, conversion rate, average order value, platform fees, production cost, and net margin. Use conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios.

4. Compliance and trust
Disclose paid promotions and affiliate relationships. Use only original or licensed material. Do not promise guaranteed income, reuse other creators' work, or publish repetitive low-value content.

5. Operating plan
Create a 90-day publishing calendar, a simple lead capture path, a customer support process, and a monthly review of revenue, costs, refunds, and audience feedback.

Final deliverable
Submit a realistic 12-month monetization model, a 90-day operating plan, and a risk register covering copyright, platform policy, taxes, and customer trust.
