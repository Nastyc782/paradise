DIGITAL SMART CLASS
YouTube Channel Growth & Digital Marketing

Growth strategy workbook

1. Positioning
- Define one audience, one transformation, and three repeatable content pillars.
- Write a channel promise that is clear in five seconds.

2. Research
- Interview five target viewers.
- Collect ten questions your audience already asks.
- Group ideas by search intent, urgency, and your ability to demonstrate a result.

3. Packaging
- Draft three honest titles and three thumbnail concepts.
- Make the first 30 seconds deliver the promise immediately.
- Use a descriptive filename, chapters, captions, and a useful description.

4. Distribution
- Publish on a consistent schedule you can maintain.
- Repurpose your own long-form ideas into short clips and community posts.
- Collaborate only with permission and disclose sponsored relationships.

5. Measurement
Track impressions, click-through rate, average view duration, returning viewers, and comments. Change one variable at a time and record the result for two publishing cycles.

Final deliverable
Submit a 30-day channel growth plan, one optimized published or unlisted video, and a one-page analytics reflection.
